Fee: Free
Eligibility: Families of children aged 18 or younger who are receiving treatment for a serious illness at Pusan National University Children’s Hospital
Application Location: Patient Administration Office, 1st Floor, Children’s Hospital (application forms available)
Duration of Use:
· Inpatients: Up to 4 weeks (may be extended up to two times)
· Outpatients: Up to 2 weeks (may be extended up to five times)

Admission Selection Criteria (Priority)
Applications are reviewed based on the following criteria rather than on a first-come, first-served basis.

· Medical Criteria: Severity of the child’s medical condition, length of hospitalization, and frequency of outpatient visits (at least twice per week)
· Geographical criteria: Distance from the family's residence to the hospital
· Family Circumstances: National or local government assistance currently received by the family
