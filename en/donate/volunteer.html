Please read before joining us

Participation Conditions

  • Group or team participation
  • Regular participation on designated days of the week
  • Interview and selection process
  • Orientation and training provided

Participation Information

  • Eligibility: Anyone with a sense of responsibility and passion.
  • Activity Type: Determined after consultation (Preference is given to applicants who can volunteer regularly.)
  • Recruitment Method: Open year-round
  • Activity Location: Varies by house

How you can help and get involved

House Operations Support

  • Guest room cleaning and bed preparation
  • Indoor and outdoor maintenance

Program and Event Support

  • Support for kimchi-making events, cooking classes, and more
  • Support for family programs and events

Professional Skills Volunteer
Opportunities

  • Lead professional training and educational programs
  • Support community service activities using your professional skills
