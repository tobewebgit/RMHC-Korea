Chairman's Greetings

We will be a source of strength by the side of the child's family.
Please support the tomorrow that the children dream of.

"Children are the Future of this Land" they often say. Helping children grow up healthy regardless of disease or environment will be the first step in advancing the future of our society.

Our foundation is working for the health and happiness of children and their families under the slogan "Family Stays". I hope that all children in this land will be supported so that they are not excluded from education due to disease, that their environment does not hinder them from nurturing their dreams, and that they will grow into the hope of our future filled with love of family.

Alone, I am weak, but the strength of us gathered 'Together' is stronger than anything. When we join hands, we can create miracles together. We thank you for visiting our website and invite you to join us in this meaningful journey for children.

The Ronald McDonald House Korea will continue to stand right by their side, becoming another family to support the healthy growth of children and the laughter of their families.

Thank you.

Ronald McDonald House Korea
Chairman Jeffrey Jones
