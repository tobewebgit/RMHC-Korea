Ronald McDonald House Korea is a strong source of support that helps protect the everyday lives of families with pediatric children

We protect the daily lives of families so that the child's family can focus solely on treatment. We stand by them as a warm source of strength at every moment of recovery.

Our promise to the families

  • Mission

    We provide essential services that remove barriers, strengthen families and promote healing when children need healthcare.

  • Vision

    A world where every family has what they need to ensure the best health outcomes for their children.

  • Slogan

We stand with pediatric families in their everyday lives

We provide support to help children undergoing treatment and their families
continue their daily lives while focusing on recovery.

  • House Operation

    We provide a warm space where children and their families can stay together.

  • Family Support

    We bring comfort, strength, and hope to children and families during treatment.

  • Education and Play

    We help children laugh, and grow even during treatment.

  • Special Program

    We convey comfort and hope through various programs.
