Gala Dinner: Creating a Brighter Night,
Creating a Brighter Tomorrow for Families

The Ronald McDonald House Gala Dinner, held at the end of each year, is our signature fundraising event supporting the construction and operation of the House. More than a fundraising event, it brings together companies and individuals to help families of children receiving treatment stay together throughout their journey. Every contribution provides warm meals, a comfortable place to stay, and hope for families when they need it most.
