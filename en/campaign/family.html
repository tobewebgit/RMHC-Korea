Every Step of McDonald’s Happy Walk Brings
Hope to Families of Children Receiving
Treatment

McDonald's Happy Walk is a participatory campaign that brings people together to support families of children receiving treatment.
Every step helps provide a place where families can stay together during treatment, offering comfort, strength,
and hope when they need it most.

Every step we take together sends encouragement to
families of children receiving treatment.

Every meaningful moment we share
together brings hope to families of
children receiving treatment.

Every step we take together and every
smile we share will become a lasting
memory of giving.

Every step of support makes a big
difference for families of children
receiving treatment.
