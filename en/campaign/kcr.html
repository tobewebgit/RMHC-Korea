Korea Charity Ride: Every Ride Brings Hope to Families of Children Receiving Treatment

Korea Charity Ride (KCR) is one of Korea's leading charity cycling events, bringing people together to support children and families in need. Ronald McDonald House Korea is proud to participate as one of KCR's beneficiary organizations, helping provide families of children receiving treatment with the care and support they need.

Every Ride Together Brings Hope to Families of Children Receiving Treatment

Courage

Covering 530 km takes the courage to push beyond one's limits. Every ride brings strength and support to families of children receiving treatment.

Hope

Every pedal stroke is a message of hope, bringing encouragement to families of children receiving treatment.

Change

KCR inspires positive change for families of children receiving treatment while advancing the value of family-centered care.
