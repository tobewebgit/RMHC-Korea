FAQ

  • Ronald McDonald House: Yangsan provides accommodations for families of pediatric patients aged 18 and under who are receiving treatment at Pusan National University Yangsan Hospital. Admission is based on the patient's treatment schedule and room availability.

  • Our rooms are designed for families to stay together. Standard occupancy is 2 to 4 guests per room, with a maximum occupancy of 3 to 5 guests depending on the room type.

  • Yes. Families who have previously stayed at the House, or those referred by the hospital, may use the House facilities during the day without an overnight stay. Availability may vary depending on House operations, so please contact us in advance.

  • The House offers a variety of spaces to help families stay comfortably during their child's treatment. Facilities include a shared kitchen and dining area, laundry room, playroom, quiet room, and massage chair lounge. We also provide programs that support families' emotional well-being, including mental health services and volunteer-led activities.

  • Donation receipts are issued in the name of the actual donor—the individual or organization that made the donation. They cannot be issued under a different name.

  • After we verify your donation and the information provided in your donation receipt request, your donation receipt will be issued through the National Tax Service (Hometax). Once issued, individual and corporate donors can view and download it directly from Hometax.

  • If required information such as your name, resident registration number, or business registration number is incorrect, we may not be able to issue a donation receipt. Please contact Ronald McDonald House Korea at rmhc@rmhc.or.kr if any correction is needed.

  • Donation receipts issued by Ronald McDonald House Korea are intended for Korean tax deduction purposes. Therefore, they are not issued to overseas donors who do not claim a Korean tax deduction.
