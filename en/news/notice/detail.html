[Announcement] 2022 Donation Receipt: Important Information for Year-End Tax Settlement

2023.01.16

We would like to inform you about the year-end tax settlement process for your donations made in 2022. You will be able to view your donation records through the National Tax Service’s simplified tax filing service starting January 15. If you notice any missing records or require any corrections, please contact us.

Available Date: Starting January 15, 2022
Inquiries: RMHC Korea (Tel: 02-723-2013 / E-mail: info@rmhc.or.kr)
