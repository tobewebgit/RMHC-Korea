[RMHC News] Ronald McDonald House: Yangsan January Arts and Crafts Workshop

2026.02.02

We have announced the results of the judging for the 10th Ullim Writing Contest.

We sincerely thank everyone who participated in this Ullim Writing Contest, and we ask for your continued interest and support so that we can deliver even greater resonance in the future.

♦ Announcement of Winners
Grand Prize - Moon Ga-hyun [Inside Out], 5th Grade, Yeomisarang Hospital School

Excellence Award - Gu Jun-seo [The Sound Coming to Me], 2nd Grade, Kyungpook National University Children's Hospital School
Excellence Award – Lee Chan-hyo [Where Shall We Go?], 3rd Grade, Seoul National University Children's Hospital School
Excellence Award – Song Ha-ru [Things That Make You Feel Better When You're Sick], 2nd Grade, Seoul St. Mary's Hospital Raphael Children's School
Special Award – Jung Hyun-seo [Precious Things That Give Me Strength], 6th Grade Elementary, Samsung Seoul Hospital School
Special Award – Jo Ji-yoon [A Dream Like Oxygen], 3rd Grade Middle School, Naju National Hospital Neutinamu Hospital School
Special Award – Jung Hyun-min [Hope, Courage], 2nd Grade High School, Yonsei Cancer Hospital School
Special Award – Moon Jung-hyun [Life and Death of the Mind], 2nd Grade High School, Naju National Hospital Neutinamu Hospital School
Special Award – Jung Su-rim [Balloon], 3rd Grade High School, Pusan National University Children's Hospital School

♦ Further details regarding this year's awards ceremony will be announced separately at a later date.
